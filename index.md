# 🌿 A Message from the UUs of Rossmoor! 🍉

## No August Meeting

Hey wonderful friends!  The Unitarian Universalists of Rossmoor *are not* meeting in August. 🤫

You might be thinking: *Did we disband? Is our spiritual journey interrupted by a lapse in poor planning? Did the universe finally decide to hit us with an existential crisis?*

The answer is much simpler (and involves slightly less profound cosmic suffering): **It is too hot.** 🔥

***

### ☀️ The Great August Hibernation 🧋

Turns out, trying to gather for deep spiritual introspection while simultaneously battling peak Rossmoor heat is a fool's errand. Instead of attempting profound thought, we collectively decided to initiate a period of strategic spiritual self-care. This involved an intense dedication to:

1.  Air Conditioning
2.  Hydrating with iced beverages
3.  Plotting our return in September

We promise, the pause is not due to any cosmic disagreement or philosophical split. We are simply recharging our communal battery packs—and planning something *delightful* for when we reconvene! 😉

***

## 🍂 Mark Your Calendars! 🎉

The Unitarian Universalists of Rossmoor will be back in September, and we're planning a welcome-back celebration. 

**You are cordially invited to our:**
### 🧺 Brown Bag Picnic Gathering! 🌳

**When:** Friday, September 11th

**Time:** 11:30 a.m.

**Where:** The Dollar Club House Patio (Easy parking and plenty of shade!)

#### What to Expect:
*   **The Vibe:** Casual. Very casual. Thinking is optional. Joy is mandatory! 😄
*   **The Food:** It's a BYOS (Bring Your Own Sandwich/Salad/Snack) situation! Don’t worry, we will have drinks and some snacks, maybe even a desert. Just bring a sandwich or a salad or whatever.
*   **The Spirit:** Wonderful company, great music, and plenty of good laughs as we toast to fall sunshine and continued growth!

***

We are looking forward to seeing all your wonderful faces. Spread the word and get ready for a relaxed afternoon that feels just right.

See you soon—with a drink in hand! 👋

**With Love and Anticipation,**

The Planning Committee UUs of Rossmoor
